EMPLOYEE SATISFACTION GUIDELINES

Employee records contain the department, satisfaction score, and tenure in years. Employee satisfaction scores range from 1.0 to 5.0.

Departments with an average satisfaction score above 4.0 demonstrate strong employee engagement. Departments with an average satisfaction score below 3.5 should receive additional management attention.

Employee satisfaction should be reviewed quarterly. Analysis may include average satisfaction by department, average tenure by department, and comparisons between departments.
