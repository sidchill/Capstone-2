CUSTOMER RETENTION POLICY

Customer records contain the customer name, industry, churn date, and satisfaction score. Satisfaction scores range from 1.0 to 5.0, where 5.0 represents the highest satisfaction.

A blank churn date means that the customer is currently active. A populated churn date indicates that the customer has churned.

Customers with satisfaction scores below 3.0 should receive priority for retention outreach. Retention analysis should consider churn by industry, average satisfaction score, and the number of active and churned customers.
